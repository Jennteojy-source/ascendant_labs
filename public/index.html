Scientific Methodology

How Flight Delays Are Verified

We don't rely on airline declarations. Aviation consumer defense relies on immutable telemetry and court precedents:

Pillar 01

Gate Arrival Timestamp Telemetry

Under European Court of Justice ruling C-452/13 (Germanwings v Henning), flight arrival is legally defined as the exact moment the aircraft doors open at the gate—not when wheels touch down.

Pillar 02

METAR Airport Weather Auditing

Airlines routinely use "bad weather" as a catch-all excuse to deny payouts. Official meteorological aerodrome reports (METAR) and radar logs are audited to prove whether neighboring flights operated normally.

Pillar 03

Technical Defect ECJ Jurisprudence

The ECJ has ruled repeatedly (C-549/07 Wallentin-Hermann) that technical malfunctions, engine wear, and component breakdowns are inherent to airline operations and do NOT constitute extraordinary circumstances.
