## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Stitching Guide for Google Flow

1. **Generate Clip 1 (0–10s):**
   - Paste **Clip 1 Prompt** into Google Veo / VideoFX.
   - Set aspect ratio to **9:16**.
   - Set duration to **10 seconds**.
   - Paste the **Clip 1 Negative Prompt**.
   - Generate and review. Ensure the 10.0s mark cleanly shows the mascot holding the tablet steady.

2. **Set Flow Continuity Anchor:**
   - In Google Flow, use the **"Flow / Extend"** feature.
   - Select the **10.0s end-frame** of Clip 1 as the initial conditioning frame for Clip 2.
   - This ensures the mascot’s hand, eye position, bottle proportions, and counter lighting remain 100% consistent across the seam.

3. **Generate Clip 2 (10–20s):**
   - Paste **Clip 2 Prompt**.
   - Google Flow will smoothly interpolate from the tablet toss into the micro-dissolve and CTA end card.

4. **Audio & Caption Assembly (CapCut, Premiere, or Descript):**
   - Place Clip 1 and Clip 2 on the video track (they will match seamlessly).
   - Generate the voice-over audio in ElevenLabs (using a warm, conversational, friendly adult voice like *Adam* or *Rachel*).
   - Align the continuous 20-second VO script:
     > *"Your mouth has its own living ecosystem. And keeping that environment balanced is everything. That's why ProDentim uses oral probiotics. One convenient chewable dissolves to support a fresh, healthy oral environment every day. Give your daily routine extra support—discover ProDentim."*
   - Add bold, uppercase dynamic captions in the safe zone (centered, with 150px margin from top and bottom).
